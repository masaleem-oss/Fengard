The filter they can't route around.

Fengard puts per-device filtering, screen time and a real firewall on the OpenWrt or GL.iNet router you already have. Every lookup from every device goes through it, because the router's kernel sends them there. Changing DNS doesn't help.

    How it works

    Every lookup goes one way.

    Fengard runs beside the router's own firmware. DHCP stays where it was. DNS, filtering and the firewall rules become Fengard's, in its own nftables table or iptables chains, and come out again if it ever stops.

    Deviceasks any DNS server
    Kernelredirects it to Fengard
    Fengardrate limit, cache, policy
    UpstreamDoH, HTTP/3 or DoT

    Features
    
      
    A family network, not just an ad blocker.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
        
    For the people on it
    
        
    
          
    Profiles
    Categories, 55 single apps like TikTok, Roblox and Fortnite, SafeSearch, schedules and their own DNS servers.
    
          
    Screen time
    A daily limit per person and per app, counted once across all their devices.
    
          
    Block page
    Says why, with a Request access button you can approve from the alert.
    
          
    My time
    fengard.lan/me on their own phone shows time left and asks for more.
    
          
    New devices
    Can wait for your approval before they get online.
    
        
    
      
    
      
    
        
    For the network
    
        
    
          
    Ads and trackers
    Gone network-wide, with HaGeZi, OISD, AdGuard and Steven Black lists one click away.
    
          
    Encrypted upstream
    DNS over TLS, HTTPS and HTTP/3, through fengard-h3, our own small QUIC client.
    
          
    Local names
    Every device answers as name.lan, and .lan never leaks upstream.
    
          
    Firewall
    Port forwards, WAN hardening, SYN and flood limits, invalid packets dropped.
    
          
    Device check
    A weekly look for open Telnet, cameras without a password and UPnP holes.
    
        
    
      
    
      
    
        
    For you
    
        
    
          
    WireGuard
    Scan a QR code and a phone gets the same filtering on mobile data.
    
          
    Alerts
    To Discord, Slack, Telegram, ntfy or a webhook, by severity.
    
          
    Accounts
    Admin and viewer roles, two-factor, API keys.
    
          
    Undo
    Every settings change is versioned, with rollback, backup and restore.
    
          
    Signed updates
    From the dashboard in about a minute, test-run first, rolled back if it doesn't start.

    Official sources
    
      
    Only from here.
    
      
    Copies of Fengard are being published under other GitHub accounts, and at least one ships
        malware instead. Fengard runs as root on your router and sees every device's traffic, so where you get it matters.
    
    
    
    
      
      
    • The code and every download live at
        github.com/masaleem-oss/Fengard. Check the account name, not just the project name.
      • 
      
    • Downloads come from its
        Releases page, never a zip in the code, and never with an .exe at the top.
      • 
      
    • Compare a download with SHA256SUMS on the same release:
        shasum -a 256 fengard-<version>.zip, or Get-FileHash on Windows.
      • 
      
    • Updates are signed with a key that isn't kept on GitHub, so a copy can't arrive through the dashboard's updater.

    FAQ
    
      
    Fair questions.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    Can kids get around it with DoH, a VPN or another DNS server?
        
    Changing DNS doesn't help: every lookup is redirected back to Fengard, and DoH, DoT, DoQ and Tor are blocked in
          the router's firewall. Known VPN and proxy services are blocked by the "VPN, proxy & DNS bypass" category.
          No filter catches every VPN, so for younger kids pair it with a schedule or a device pause.
    
      
    Do I have to flash my router?
        
    No. If it already runs OpenWrt or GL.iNet firmware, Fengard installs next to it and uninstalls cleanly. If it
          runs stock firmware that isn't OpenWrt-based, run Fengard on an always-on computer instead, or flash OpenWrt.
    
      
    Will it slow my internet down?
        
    No. Only DNS lookups go through Fengard, answered in milliseconds and mostly from cache. Everything else is plain
          kernel routing.
    
      
    What happens if Fengard stops?
        
    A small watchdog checks it every 20 seconds. If it's stopped, crashing or missing, plain dnsmasq takes over DNS
          within about a minute and a half and Fengard's firewall rules come out. Filtering is off while that happens, but
          nobody loses internet.
    
      
    What about phones with private Wi-Fi addresses?
        
    Most keep one private address per network. When one changes, Fengard recognises it from its DHCP name and
          fingerprint, the old address going quiet, and the sites it uses, and puts it back in its profile. Guesses it
          isn't sure about wait for you, and a copied name is never enough.
    
      
    Is it a Pi-hole or AdGuard Home alternative?
        
    Yes. It blocks ads, trackers and malware network-wide like they do, and adds screen time, app blocking, a block
          page and firewall enforcement. If AdGuard Home is on the router, the installer offers to turn it off and
          removing Fengard turns it back on.
